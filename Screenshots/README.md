### Screenshot 1 — Funnel Overview Dashboard
This page provides a high-level overview of the marketing campaign funnel and overall conversion performance. The dashboard highlights total customers contacted, total conversions, conversion rate, customer drop-offs, monthly conversion trends, and the impact of contact frequency on customer conversion behavior. Interactive slicers allow users to filter the analysis by month, job role, and age group to support dynamic business analysis and campaign performance evaluation.

### Screenshot 2 — Customer Segment Analysis Dashboard
This page focuses on customer segmentation and conversion analysis across different demographic groups. The dashboard evaluates customer conversion performance by age group, job role, marital status, education level, and previous campaign outcomes. The analysis helps identify high-performing customer segments, understand customer behavior patterns, and support more targeted marketing strategies.
